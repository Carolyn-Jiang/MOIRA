PAIR TRADING DIRECTION DISCOVERY TASK

Pair Selection has already been completed. Do not choose a new pair.

You are now deciding how to trade the active pair today.

In pair trading, direction is about relative performance between Asset A and Asset B.

Actions:

LONG_SHORT:
- long Asset A
- short Asset B
- use this when Asset A is expected to outperform Asset B, or when the spread suggests Asset A is cheap relative to Asset B and likely to revert upward.

SHORT_LONG:
- short Asset A
- long Asset B
- use this when Asset B is expected to outperform Asset A, or when the spread suggests Asset A is rich relative to Asset B and likely to revert downward.

FLAT:
- hold cash
- use only when the evidence is weak, contradictory, or lifecycle constraints make trading unsafe.

Your objective is to maximize risk-adjusted return, not to minimize trading activity.

You must consider:

1. current spread state,
2. long-horizon z-score,
3. trading-window z-score,
4. today's prices,
5. today's price deltas: P_T - P_{T-1},
6. latest available news,
7. relative news between Asset A and Asset B,
8. company business context,
9. economic relationship between the companies,
10. past 3 days Direction Memory,
11. lifecycle state.

Do not analyze Asset A and Asset B independently. Compare them.

Ask:

- Which asset is likely to outperform the other today?
- Does the spread suggest mean reversion or continued divergence?
- Does today's price delta confirm or contradict the spread signal?
- Did recent memory show that similar decisions were right or wrong?
- Did the counterfactual actions outperform the chosen action in recent days?

Lifecycle state is contextual information only; it is never an action constraint. WARNING means the pair relationship is weakening but not confirmed broken; it must not force FLAT or prevent trading. BREAK means the pair has failed pair-quality validation and will be replaced at the next pair-selection opportunity; it warrants extra caution, but it does NOT force FLAT, does NOT prohibit trading, and does NOT replace your decision with a fixed action. Under any lifecycle state, choose one of LONG_SHORT / SHORT_LONG / FLAT using the same process: spread state, price movement, news, and recent memory.

FLAT is valid, but repeated FLAT decisions should not become the default policy. If there is a clear relative edge, choose LONG_SHORT or SHORT_LONG.
