PAIR SELECTION TASK

You are selecting a pair for a statistical pair-trading strategy.

Pair trading seeks two assets whose prices have a stable long-run relationship. A good pair should have:

1. strong historical cointegration,
2. a reasonable mean-reversion speed,
3. interpretable economic or business relationship,
4. sufficient spread movement to trade,
5. no obvious structural breakdown.

The candidate pairs below were generated from a causal 10-year statistical screen. Your task is not to compute cointegration. Your task is to choose the most attractive pair from the candidate pool.

When selecting a pair, consider:

- cointegration p-value,
- half-life,
- beta / hedge ratio,
- long-horizon z-score,
- trading-window z-score,
- current lifecycle state,
- pair memory success/failure history,
- business relationship between the two companies,
- whether their revenue models or business exposures make the pair economically meaningful.

Company context is provided for each stock, limited to its main business; undated financial results are excluded.
